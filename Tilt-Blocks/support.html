How do I play?

Drag pieces from the tray onto the 8×8 board. Fill a whole row or column and it clears. The game ends when none of your pieces fit and you have no tilts left. You can replay the tutorial any time: open Settings (the gear on the home screen) and tap How to play.

What does tilting do?

Tilting turns the whole board on its side. The wall you tilt toward becomes the new floor, and every block falls down onto it. Rows a tilt fills up clear and are worth double points. Press and hold a tilt button to preview where every block will land, then let go to tilt.

How do I get more tilts?

You start each game with 1 tilt. Every 3 lines you clear earns another, and you can hold up to 3 at a time. When a game ends you can also watch an optional short video to get one more tilt and keep playing.

The game says “No room for your pieces”

None of your pieces fit, but you still have a tilt. Tilt the board left or right: everything packs against one wall, which usually makes space.

Why are there ads?

Ads keep Tilt Blocks free. You’ll see a banner below the game and an occasional ad between games, never in the middle of a game. Reward videos are always optional.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

On iOS, tracking can be turned on or off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, open the game’s Settings and tap Privacy choices. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I reset my progress?

Your best score and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app resets them.
