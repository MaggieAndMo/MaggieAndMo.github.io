Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what information the Tilt Blocks mobile game (“the app,” “we”) handles, and the choices you have. The short version: the game keeps your data on your device, has no accounts, and the only third-party data collection comes from the advertising service described below.

Information we do not collect

Tilt Blocks has no user accounts and no sign-in. Your gameplay data (best score, games played, whether you finished the tutorial, and your sound and vibration settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner below the game, an occasional full-screen ad between games, and optional reward videos. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect:

  • Device identifiers (on iOS, the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when asked; see “App Tracking Transparency” below)
  • IP address and coarse (city-level) location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, operating system version, app version)
  • Ad interaction data (which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped) and diagnostic data

How Google uses this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy.

App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, the app asks for your permission before any cross-app tracking, using Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, the app still works fully and ads still appear; they are simply less personalized. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Your consent choices (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app shows a consent message (Google’s certified consent management platform) before serving ads, where you can consent, decline, or manage options per ad partner. If you decline, the ads shown are limited and non-personalized. You can change your choices at any time from Settings → Privacy choices inside the app.

Optional reward videos

Watching a video ad is always optional. When a game ends, it can give you one extra tilt to keep playing. Every part of the game can be played without ever watching one.

US state privacy rights

We do not sell your personal information ourselves. Our advertising partner (Google) may process data as described above. Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws can exercise opt-out and access or deletion rights through the consent and tracking controls described in this policy, or by contacting us.

Children

Tilt Blocks is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app’s ad services, contact us and we will help address it.

Security and retention

We keep no servers and hold no user databases for this app. Data handled by Google for advertising is retained under Google’s policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its “Last updated” date.
